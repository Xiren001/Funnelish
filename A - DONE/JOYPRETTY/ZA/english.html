I was told that female facial hair is caused by some kind of hormonal problem.

That's not entirely true.

All women have testosterone, and contrary to popular belief, it is not what causes facial hair.

The real culprit is an enzyme hiding in your hair follicles called 5-alpha-reductase…

This enzyme takes your NORMAL hormones… and converts them into dihydrotestosterone (DHT) – which is basically rocket fuel for hair growth.

Think of your hair follicle as a little factory. It takes the building blocks of protein and assembles them into hair.

When DHT shows up, it takes over that factory... and forces it to produce thick, dark hair. Fast.
