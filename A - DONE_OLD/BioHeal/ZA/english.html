So what is it about?

Intertrigo is a fungal and bacterial infection that occurs in skin folds where moisture is trapped between skin surfaces.

It appears as raw, red, oozing patches that smell like yeast or rotting meat. The affected skin becomes macerated (moist and white), develops painful cracks, and burns with every movement.

Up to 1 in 5 women over the age of 40 experience chronic intertrigo.

Who is at risk?

  • Women during and after menopause (hormonal changes)

  • People who are overweight and have skin folds

  • People with diabetes and prediabetes (high blood sugar feeds yeast)

  • Pregnant women (increased sweating and skin folds)

  • People who live in hot, humid climates

  • Anyone who sweats a lot

  • People who sit at a desk for work
