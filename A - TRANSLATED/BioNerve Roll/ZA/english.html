BioEdge™ restores oxygen supply, calms the nerves and reduces pain within a few hours.

Oxygen reaches your body through the bloodstream, which carries it from the lungs to the heart and then distributes it throughout the rest of the body.

When we're young, that system works perfectly. But as we get older, something changes.

And because of that, your nerves get damaged due to poor blood flow — and that's how neuropathy develops.

As we age, our bodies retain less water, resulting in less fluid in our bloodstream and a decrease in blood volume.

And with less blood volume, it becomes much harder for the heart to pump enough oxygen to all the cells — especially to the extremities, such as the feet.

For people with diabetes, for example, it is even worse.

High blood sugar makes blood vessels less flexible. They become stiffer, contract, and further restrict blood flow.

Without enough oxygen, nerve cells become damaged and slowly begin to die.

This is what causes that burning pain, that unbearable tingling, and that numbness — exactly what so many people with neuropathy experience every day.

I'll say it again…

The nerve cells in your feet are literally slowly being 'strangled' because they are not getting enough oxygen from the blood.
